->So now what will happen, we will have our DBMS in the RAM, which will have its own storage buffer or buffer pool or technically saying page cache. What will happen, the same thing, like DBMS is like a process in RAM. The file, from the file, the pages will be copied to the kernel's page cache, and from the page cache, it will be copied to the DBMS buffer pool or the storage buffer. And now from now, what all the operations that require the SQL will happen through the storage buffer of the DBMS, not the OS. So we will have full control on how the eviction policy and everything will be, and that can be optimized.
